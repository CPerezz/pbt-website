EIP-8297 · storage layout · 2026-10

The first 64 slots

EIP-8297 keeps every account's storage slots 0–63 inside its header. We measured on seven months of mainnet what that buys today, what it could buy with a gas rule that treats the header as one unit, and whether 64 is the right number.
